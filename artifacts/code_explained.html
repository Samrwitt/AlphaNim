Compute the cross-entropy/softmax derivative with respect to action logits: (predicted probability - target) divided by batch size. Despite its short name, dp is the derivative at the logits. The tiny logarithm safeguard causes a negligible approximation, checked numerically below.

Follow one move

At state 10, A removes 2. Record (10, 2, a), reduce stones to 8, then switch to B. If A ultimately wins, the labeled row is (10, 2, 1, a). Encode state 10 with a 1 in slot ten; action 2 has target [0, 1]; outcome target is 1.

For probabilities [0.6, 0.4] and value 0.2, this row contributes approximately −log(0.4) + (0.2 − 1)² = 1.5563 to the loss. Before batch averaging, action-logit derivatives are [0.6, −0.6] and the outcome preactivation derivative is −1.536. Backpropagate these, then subtract learning rate times each parameter gradient.

Random-action prediction learns the random distribution. This exercise does not claim to be full AlphaZero.
