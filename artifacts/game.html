How AlphaZero learned · training results

Both players used the same policy-and-value network plus Monte Carlo tree search. Search visit fractions taught the policy; the final game outcome taught the value. After each batch of 30 games, our own backpropagation and gradient descent updated the weights. Exactly 300 self-play games were used, starting from random network weights.

Training games include exploration and earlier versions of the agent. When you play, you face the final trained model, with exploration disabled. Your games run locally and do not change the 300-game training dataset.
